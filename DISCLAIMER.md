# Disclaimer

## Untested

Every design in this repository is labelled **untested, v0.1**. The designs have
been modelled in CAD, inspected in CAD, and analysed in simulation. **None of
them has been printed, assembled, worn, handled, or tested by a person.**

Simulation results are predictions produced from an idealised solid model using
assumed material properties and assumed loads. A printed part is not the same
object as the solid model: layer adhesion makes FDM parts anisotropic and
typically weaker in the Z direction than the bulk material values used in
analysis. Treat every reported safety factor as a design-stage indicator, not as
a statement about a printed part.

Inspect any printed part before use. A part that fails under load can pinch,
drop a hot or sharp object, or cause a fall. Do not use these parts in any
situation where failure could cause injury.
